## Pricing decision

The primary Atlanta franchise page publishes sizes/dimensions but no itemized dollar prices and directs customers toward a quote/contact path. The live booking flow was verified through address → franchise match → size selection; no price was shown in the reached steps.

A secondary/legacy domain associated with the Atlanta franchise publishes a `$400–$485/week` range depending on size, but the range is not itemized by product and is not corroborated on the primary Atlanta domain.

**V1 public treatment:** BTDT comparable product cards should show **Price requires quote**. Do not derive or allocate the `$400–$485` range across 10/15/20-yard products.

## Internal-only evidence / unresolved conflicts

- Secondary-domain range: $400–$485/week, not size-itemized.
- Secondary-domain included tonnage: 10 yd 1.5 tons; 15 yd 2.5 tons; 20 yd 3.5 tons. Single-source, not corroborated on primary domain.
- Extra day: $20/day on secondary domain.
- Overage wording conflicts: `$100/ton using quarter-ton increments` vs another page describing pound-level proration.
- Franchise duration: secondary franchise page says 7 days; national corporate content describes 5–7 days.
- Contact details conflict across Atlanta-franchise web properties: three phone numbers and two physical addresses.
- Atlanta East publishes itemized prices but does not serve the target markets; those figures are internal reference only.
